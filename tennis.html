Tennis

Tennis has shaped me in every way — from waking up early to practice in Kazakhstan, to competing in national tournaments, and now representing Northern Kentucky University. Every practice has taught me something: resilience, discipline, and joy.

  • 👧 Age 5: Started tennis in my hometown in Kazakhstan
  • 🏆 Age 8: Went to my first tournament
  • 🌍 Age 13: Played my first international match
  • 🎓 2022–Now: Competing for NKU in U.S. collegiate tennis

🏅 Leadership on and off the court

Tennis is a lonely sport. But Bbeing in a team leader taught me to communicate, motivate, and lead. Whether organizing team practices, encouraging teammates, or staying positive through tough matches, I've learned that leadership in tennis isn’t just about performance — it’s about character!
